Clipador - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Clipador is a personal tool operated by its owner. This policy explains what data it handles when a TikTok account is connected.

Data we access

Only from accounts that connect through Login with TikTok:

  • Basic profile: open ID, display name, username and avatar (scopes user.info.basic, user.info.profile), to show which account each clip goes to.
  • Public video list (scope video.list), to check what was already posted.
  • Upload and publish permissions (scopes video.upload, video.publish), to send the owner's own clips as drafts or private posts.

How it is stored

Access tokens are kept by our integration provider (Composio) and are never shared. Profile names and the scheduling history are stored locally on the operator's computer.

What we do not do

We do not sell, share or use data for advertising; we do not collect data from viewers or from other TikTok users; we do not post anything that was not approved by a person.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the account stays connected. Removing the account in Clipador, or revoking access in the TikTok app (Settings > Security > Apps and services), deletes the access token. To ask for deletion of any remaining data, write to the contact below.
