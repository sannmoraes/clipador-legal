Clipador - Terms of Service

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. What Clipador is

Clipador is a personal tool operated by its owner to schedule and upload short video clips to TikTok accounts that the operator owns or manages.

2. Who may use it

Only the operator and accounts the operator explicitly authorizes. It is not a public service and no sign-up is offered.

3. TikTok accounts

By connecting a TikTok account through "Login with TikTok", the account owner allows Clipador to upload videos to that account's inbox as drafts (or as private posts) and to read basic profile information (display name, username, avatar) and the list of public videos. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings > Security > Apps and services) or by removing the account in Clipador.

4. Content

The account owner is responsible for the content uploaded and for following TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service. Every clip is reviewed and approved by a person before it is scheduled; nothing is posted without that approval.

5. No warranty

Clipador is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. The operator is not liable for failed uploads, TikTok API changes or account actions taken by TikTok.
