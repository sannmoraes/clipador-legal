Clipador

Clipador is a personal, self-hosted tool used by its owner to cut short clips from gaming videos and live streams, review them by hand, and schedule them for upload to the owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

Clips are sent to TikTok through the TikTok Content Posting API, as drafts in the creator's inbox (or as private posts). Every clip is approved manually before it is scheduled. Clipador is not offered to the public and does not collect data from other TikTok users.
